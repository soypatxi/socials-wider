Getting started

  1. Swipe through the backgrounds and choose Continue.
  2. Select the social websites you want and choose Let’s go.
  3. Tap a social icon to open its website. Sign in on that website if needed.

Use the Customize button at the top of the home screen to change your background or selected websites.

Common questions

How do I return to Socials Wider?

Touch and hold with two fingers on a website, then choose Return to Wider in Website options. VoiceOver users can use the website view's Browser options or Return to Wider accessibility action. Socials Wider does not automatically configure iPhone Back Tap.

Can I open another website?

Yes. Open Website options with a two-finger hold. Under Open a website, enter a domain or an http:// or https:// address, then choose Go. A domain without a scheme uses HTTPS. For an address with a custom port, include http:// or https://. Unencrypted HTTP pages are marked Not Secure. The browser also follows ordinary links on the websites you visit.

How do I add the widget?

On your iPhone Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, choose Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Socials Wider, choose a widget size and add it. Its icons use the websites and background you selected in the app. A widget opens the app's browser; it does not run a website over other apps.

Why does sign-in ask for my password or a security code?

You are signing in to the website. Its operator decides which sign-in and verification steps are needed. Socials Wider cannot import a session from another app or Safari, read your Apple Passwords vault, bypass verification or guarantee a biometric prompt. Apple's password suggestions and passkeys depend on the website, your saved accounts and system support.

Complete any verification directly on the genuine website. We will never ask you to send a password or security code to support.

Will an update sign me out?

The app uses WebKit's persistent website store and does not intentionally clear it during an ordinary update. A website can still expire or revoke a session. Deleting the app, changing to a different app or changes made by the website can require you to sign in again.

A website looks wrong or a feature does not work.

Open Website options with a two-finger hold and try Reload. You can also choose Open outside Wider to use another browser. Website layouts and supported features can change independently of Socials Wider. Let us know which website and what you were doing, without including private account content.

How do I sign out or delete my information?

Sign out through the website's own menu. To delete a website account or content, use that website's privacy and account controls. Socials Wider does not create an account for you.

Deleting Socials Wider using iOS's Delete App option removes its local app and browser data; offloading keeps that data. Device backups are managed through Apple's settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Is Socials Wider made by the social platforms?

No. It is an independent browser made by F2F Interactive Ltd. Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube operate their own websites, accounts and services. Their availability and website features may differ from their native apps.
