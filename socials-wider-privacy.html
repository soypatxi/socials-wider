Privacy policy · Updated 17 November 2026

Socials Wider is an independent browser with quick access to social websites. You can follow ordinary web links or enter another website address in Website options. You do not need a Socials Wider account. Your selected websites and background stay on your device.

Who we are. F2F Interactive Ltd develops Socials Wider and is responsible for the app's own data practices. Contact us at help@f2finteractive.com. The websites you visit are operated independently and have their own accounts, privacy policies and data practices.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves your chosen social websites, bundled background and whether you have completed setup. It shares those preferences with its Home Screen widget using an Apple app group. The widget does not receive your passwords, website content or website sessions.

Apple's WebKit browser framework stores website cookies, cached files and other website data inside the app so websites can work and remember your sign-in. This is a normal, persistent browser store, not a private-browsing mode. Website data is separate from the app's wallpaper and widget preferences. We do not upload your app configuration or a browsing-history database to F2F Interactive.

When you visit a website

Opening a website connects your device to that website and the services it loads. Those services can receive ordinary browser information, including your IP address, the requested address, browser and device information, cookies, and information you choose to enter, view, upload or submit. This can include account details, messages, searches, photos, videos and interactions with the site.

The website operator controls its content, advertisements, tracking technologies, account requirements, security checks and retention policies. Use the website's privacy controls and account settings to manage those practices. Socials Wider does not replace them or make a website anonymous. We do not sell your selected websites or wallpaper preferences to advertisers, and we do not include a native advertising, analytics or social-login SDK in the app.

The app adjusts the presentation of supported websites on your device, for example their width, navigation and media layout. It does not send the page content to a F2F Interactive server for those adjustments.

Sign-in, Passwords and biometrics

You sign in directly to the website, not to F2F Interactive. Website sessions are kept by WebKit. Socials Wider does not receive a copy of the passwords saved in Apple Passwords and cannot read your Face ID or Touch ID data. Where available, password suggestions, passkeys and biometric approval are provided by Apple's system and the website. They are not guaranteed on every site. A website may still ask for verification codes or other security checks.

We do not capture, log or transmit your entered website passwords, verification codes or session cookies to F2F Interactive.

Camera, microphone and uploads

A website may request camera or microphone access for a feature you choose to use. WebKit and iOS present the permission request; the app does not grant access automatically. Media you share through a website is sent to that website, not to our own media service. If you choose a file or photo in a system picker, that selection is made available to the requesting website.

You can decline these requests and continue using features that do not need them. You can change the app's camera or microphone permission in your iPhone's Settings. Website account and upload controls remain with the website operator.

Support and our website

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer your request and investigate a problem you report. Do not send passwords, verification codes, private messages or other people's personal information. A device model, iOS version and app version are normally enough to start troubleshooting.

These privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes normal connection information and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. These pages do not include our own analytics or advertising scripts. Our email and hosting providers process information to deliver those services; we do not use support correspondence to build a profile of your social browsing.

Apple diagnostics

Apple may process app and device diagnostics according to your device, App Store and TestFlight settings. If you choose to share them, Apple may make crash reports, diagnostic information or beta feedback available to us through its developer services. We use reports made available to us to investigate faults and improve reliability. This is separate from a native analytics SDK; Socials Wider does not add one. Manage optional sharing through Apple's settings and services.

Retention, deletion and your choices

  • App preferences: change your websites or background using Customize in Socials Wider. Local app data remains on the device until removed by you or iOS.
  • Website sign-ins: sign out using the website's own controls. Websites decide when their sessions expire. The app does not currently include a separate clear-browser-data button.
  • Removing local data: deleting the app using iOS's Delete App option removes its local app and browser data. Offloading the app keeps its data. This does not delete your accounts or content held by websites.
  • Device backups: iOS may include app data in device backups according to your Apple settings. Restoring a backup may restore app data. Manage those backups through Apple's settings and services.
  • Website accounts and content: use the website's account, privacy and deletion controls, or contact that operator. F2F Interactive cannot delete another company's account or server records for you.
  • Support correspondence: we retain it while needed to deal with your request, maintain relevant security records or meet legal obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold about you.
  • Apple diagnostics and feedback: Apple controls availability through its services. If we retain a report for an investigation, we keep it only while needed to resolve the issue or meet relevant legal obligations.

Children

Socials Wider is not designed for children. Each website has its own minimum age and account rules. Follow the age rating shown for the app and the requirements of any website you choose to use.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, contact help@f2finteractive.com.
